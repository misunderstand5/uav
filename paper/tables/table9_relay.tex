\begin{flushleft}\footnotesize
User attachment is identical for all methods ($J=0.9992$) because attachment is fixed by the shared ROCS-0 weighted-refill scheduler rather than by the learned controller. The proposed method shows the lowest $J(\mathrm{power})=0.8475$: it deliberately concentrates NOMA backhaul power on the backhaul-limited relays 1--2 ($1.95/1.57$~W vs.\ $0.76/0.71$~W for relays 3--4); even so relays 1--2 remain backhaul-limited (load ratio $1.23/1.15$) while relays 3--4 operate below capacity ($0.83/0.78$), and per-relay QSR stays balanced ($J=0.9975$; per-relay $0.762$--$0.854$). TD3 keeps power nearly uniform ($J=0.9932$) while all four of its relays remain backhaul-congested (load ratio $\geq 1.15$) and its per-relay QSR is uniformly lower ($0.749$--$0.787$). See Fig.~7.
\end{flushleft}
